📌 how to play

Keystroke Keep lives in the strip at the bottom of your screen.

  1. Type & click anywhere. That makes Sparks ⚡ and builds Fervor.
  2. Sparks fire your heroes' ultimates automatically.
  3. Fervor makes the party stronger. Reach FLOW STATE!
  4. Spend gold in ⚔ Party to level up.
  5. Bosses every 10 floors drop Relics.
  6. Stuck? ♛ Retire for permanent Renown.
  7. Click the golden rat when it scurries by!

We only count keystrokes. We never record what you type.
